Class Part2CardCostRender

Modifies how card costs are rendered in Act 2 to add support for mixed costs and custom costs. Also reduces the size of card costs so they take up less space on the card.

CombineVanillaCostTextures(List<Texture2D>, Texture2D, bool, int)

A variant of TextureHelper.CombineTextures specifically designed for combining vanilla cost textures. Each cost texture is assumed to be 13 pixels in height and UP TO 24 pixels in width, and this method compensates for any textures thinner than 24 px.
